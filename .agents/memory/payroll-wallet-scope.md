---
name: Payroll wallet scope
description: Product positioning and unresolved decisions from the November 25, 2026 Payments meeting.
---

The Payments product centers on remote workers receiving USDC and choosing when and how much to convert to local currency. Payroll providers and employers of record are potential pilot and distribution partners; the first segment and geography still need validation.

**Why:** The user requested UI updates based on the November 25, 2026 meeting notes, which distinguish this wallet from generic business payouts.

**How to apply:** Prioritize the worker wallet and optional partial conversion in product messaging. Do not imply that an employer payroll run automatically converts workers' balances.

Custody and key management are not decided. Yield, job-loss insurance, medical insurance and short-term credit are exploratory additions, not available or approved services.

**Why:** The meeting explicitly leaves custody, providers, demand and regulatory questions open.

**How to apply:** Do not claim self-custody, guaranteed returns, live coverage, approved lending or confirmed integrations. Label exploratory benefit previews accordingly. Keep the separate Africa trade-infrastructure opportunity distinct from the payroll wallet.
